---
title: Security model
description: What is protected, what is not, and how to report a problem.
---

- **Keys:** bearer secrets stored as hashes; shown once; scoped; optionally IP-restricted and expiring. Keep them on servers only.
- **Data at rest:** message payloads, inbound bodies and attachments, SMTP passwords, webhook secrets and DNS provider tokens are sealed with `ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Rotate it with `rekey`.
- **Data in the API:** status responses never include variables, tokens or the recipient. Webhook payloads carry ids and states only.
- **Webhooks:** signed, with a replay window; SSRF-guarded on every connection; redirects never followed.
- **Operator API:** `/admin/v1` is for the dashboard and operators with a session and CSRF protection. Do not publish it, and do not route this documentation site to it.
- **Mail:** managed submission uses mutual TLS and fails closed without the DKIM signer; there is no open relay.
- **Delivery:** the system cannot prove delivery and says so everywhere.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue with exploit details. Contact the operator of your instance, and the maintainers through the repository's security contact.
