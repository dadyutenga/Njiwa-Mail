---
title: Marketing mail
description: Contacts, lists, segments, campaigns, unsubscribes and tracking, and how they stay apart from transactional mail.
---

Njiwa Mail sends transactional mail (codes, receipts, resets) and marketing mail (newsletters, announcements) from
the same installation, but keeps them apart so a marketing problem never stops a password reset.

## Audience

- **Contacts** are people who agreed to receive your marketing mail. Their address and name are encrypted; the
  dashboard shows a masked address. **Attributes** (such as `plan` or `city`) are plain values used for segments and
  as merge fields, so do not put secrets in them.
- **Lists** are static audiences. Import a CSV or JSON file (check it first; nothing is saved until you import), add
  people one by one, or let your application add sign-ups with [`POST /v1/contacts`](/docs/api/contacts).
- **Segments** are rules (status, list, domain, attribute, date added), worked out again when a campaign is created.
- A contact's **status** comes from what sending already knows: `bounced` and `complained` (a suppression),
  `unsubscribed`, or `active`. Only active contacts receive campaigns.

## Campaigns

A campaign (Bulk send) goes to pasted addresses, a list or a segment. It must come from a sender on a verified
**bulk domain** (for example `news.yourdomain`), is paced at its own rate behind transactional mail, never uses the
transactional quota, and pauses itself on a spam complaint or a hard-bounce rate above 5%.

## Unsubscribes

Every campaign message has a `List-Unsubscribe` header with one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) and a link in the body.

| From | The link | Also offered |
|---|---|---|
| A list campaign | Leaves that list | "All marketing mail" |
| Any other campaign | Leaves all marketing mail of the environment | |

Each unsubscribe sends an `email.unsubscribed` [webhook](/docs/webhooks). Transactional mail is never affected, and
resubscribing someone (dashboard only) never lifts a bounce, a complaint or a block.

## Tracking

Open and click tracking is **off** unless a campaign turns it on, and only for written messages (a template is sent as
it is). Clicks go through a redirect that only leads to the links stored with the campaign. No IP address or user agent
is kept. Opens are an estimate: some mail apps load images on their own (for example Apple Mail Privacy Protection) and
others block them, so use clicks and replies to judge a campaign.
