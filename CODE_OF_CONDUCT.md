# Code of Conduct

Njiwa Mail is built by people with different languages, countries and levels of experience. We want taking part to be a good
experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in English or Kiswahili. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain the reason behind a review comment.
- Welcome newcomers. A first contribution, a basic question or a beginner's mistake is welcome here.
- Respect privacy: do not post anyone's personal data, private messages or production details without their consent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, unwelcome attention, intimidation, or threats.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/dadyutenga/Njiwa-Mail/security/advisories/new) with "Code of Conduct" in the title.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, or temporarily or permanently ban,
anyone who breaks this code.
